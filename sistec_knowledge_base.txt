==== DOCUMENT: About Sagar Group of Institutions ====
SOURCE: sistec.ac.in/about

Sagar Group of Institutions (SGI) is a premier educational conglomerate founded in 2007, located in Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh, India. It is managed by Shri Agrawal Educational & Welfare Society. SGI has grown into one of the most respected groups of technical and professional institutions in Central India.

SGI operates multiple colleges under its umbrella:
1. SISTec – Sagar Institute of Science & Technology (Flagship engineering institute)
2. SISTec-R – Sagar Institute of Science, Technology & Research (Research and innovation hub)
3. SISTec-E – Sagar Institute of Science, Technology & Engineering
4. SIPTec – Sagar Institute of Pharmacy & Technology (Pharmacy education)
5. SIPTec-R – Sagar Institute of Pharmaceutical Technology & Research
6. SISTec Business School – MBA and management education

Vision: To become an institution of excellence in Science & Technology.
Mission: To prioritize students and provide them with a holistic education.

==== DOCUMENT: Student Rules and Code of Conduct ====
SOURCE: sistec.ac.in/student-rulebook

STUDENT CODE OF CONDUCT – SISTec

Academic Rules:
1. Minimum 75% attendance is mandatory in all subjects to be eligible to sit in university examinations.
2. Students must carry their identity card at all times on campus.
3. Use of mobile phones is not allowed in classrooms, labs, and library.
4. Ragging is strictly prohibited as per UGC Anti-Ragging guidelines. Any form of ragging will lead to immediate expulsion.
5. Examination malpractice (copying, use of unfair means) will lead to cancellation of examination and may lead to rustication.
6. Students must maintain discipline and decorum in classrooms and campus premises.
7. Submission of assignments and projects must be done within the stipulated time.

Hostel Rules:
1. Hostel in-time: 9:00 PM for Boys; 8:00 PM for Girls
2. Visitors are allowed only in the designated visiting area during visiting hours (3 PM – 5 PM)
3. Consumption of alcohol, tobacco, or any intoxicants is strictly prohibited inside the hostel
4. Cooking inside hostel rooms is not allowed
5. Students must maintain cleanliness in their rooms and common areas
6. Any damage to hostel property will be recovered from the student

Disciplinary Action:
- Written warning for minor offenses
- Fine as per offense category
- Suspension for repeated violations
- Expulsion for serious violations (ragging, substance abuse, violence)

Grievance Redressal:
- Students can submit grievances to the Student Grievance Cell
- Anti-Ragging Helpline: Available on college website
- Women's Cell for gender-related grievances

==== DOCUMENT: Examination and Academic Calendar ====
SOURCE: sistec.ac.in/academics

Academic Structure:
- Semester System: Even and Odd Semesters
- Odd Semester: July – November (Exams in November)
- Even Semester: December – April (Exams in April/May)
- Summer Training/Internship: May – June (for 3rd year students)

Internal Assessment:
- 2 Unit Tests per semester (each carrying 20 marks)
- Assignment/Practical marks
- Internal assessment carries 30% of total marks

University Examinations:
- Conducted by RGPV Bhopal
- End-semester exams carry 70% of total marks
- Practical exams conducted internally but moderated by university examiners

Grading System (RGPV):
- O (Outstanding): 91-100 → 10 grade points
- A+ (Excellent): 81-90 → 9 grade points
- A (Very Good): 71-80 → 8 grade points
- B+ (Good): 61-70 → 7 grade points
- B (Above Average): 51-60 → 6 grade points
- C (Average): 41-50 → 5 grade points
- F (Fail): Below 40 → 0 grade points

Backlog/Arrear:
- Students can appear in ex-student examinations for clearing backlogs
- Maximum 8 years allowed to complete B.Tech degree

==== DOCUMENT: Anti-Ragging Policy ====
SOURCE: sistec.ac.in/anti-ragging

Anti-Ragging Policy at SISTec:
SISTec follows the UGC Anti-Ragging guidelines strictly.

Definition: Ragging is any act that causes physical or psychological harm to a student.

Consequences of Ragging:
- Suspension from the institution
- Expulsion and cancellation of admission
- Filing of FIR with police
- Debarring from other institutions

Reporting:
- Anti-Ragging Helpline (National): 1800-180-5522
- Anti-Ragging Committee at SISTec is constituted each year
- Online complaint: antiragging.in
- Students and parents can report incidents confidentially

Every student and parent must sign the Anti-Ragging undertaking at the time of admission.
